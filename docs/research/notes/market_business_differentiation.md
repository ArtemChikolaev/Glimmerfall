# Business landscape for a new indie pixel-art action roguelite (2025–2026): market, go-to-market, platforms, monetization, co-op, differentiation

Research date: 2026-10-25. All sales and revenue numbers are **estimates unless the developer or publisher announced them**. The estimate source is named each time: Raijin, Alinea Analytics, GameDiscoverCo (GDCo), VG Insights, and AppMagic are third-party trackers. Several primary pages blocked direct fetching (SteamDB, Destructoid, gamedev.net, Push Square, Raijin; all returned HTTP 403). For those, figures come from search-engine summaries of the page or from secondary write-ups, and are flagged. The shared web-search budget ran out before every lead could be followed; what is missing is listed under Gaps. "Inferences" are my reasoning, not sourced facts.

## 1. Market 2024–2026: size, saturation, breakouts and flops

### Takeaway
Roguelites are one of Steam's fastest-growing and most crowded genres. The number of tagged releases grows every year: about 1.4k in 2024, about 1.8k in 2025, and about 2.1k in the first ten months of 2026. Alinea estimates genre revenue at about $400M on Steam in 2025, up 80%. Results are extremely hit-driven: a few breakouts each year sell 1–5M+ copies, while many releases get almost no reviews. Pure survivors-likes are late in their genre cycle (an oligopoly). Since late 2025 the breakouts have come from (a) a sharp twist on a familiar frame (Megabonk = 3D survivors; Ball x Pit = brick-breaker + survivors + base building; Balatro and CloverPit = poker or slots + roguelike), (b) trusted studios and sequels (Hades II, Slay the Spire 2, Mewgenics), or (c) co-op.

- **All of Steam:** about 20,000 games were released in 2025, and almost half have fewer than 10 reviews ([GDCo via GameDev Reports, Dec 29, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-most-successful-new)).
- **Genre revenue (estimate):** Alinea estimates roguelikes grossed about $400M on Steam in 2025, up 80% on 2024, and were approaching $500M for 2026 by May 14, 2026. Roguelike deckbuilders over $1M averaged only $3.4M, among the lowest of any subgenre ([Game World Observer, Jul 1, 2025](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/07/01/analytics-three-quarters-of-game-revenue-on-steam-comes-from-action-and-rpgs)).

#### The survivors-like cycle and fatigue
- **Zukowski's four-phase genre cycle** (Nov 12, 2025): proto game → genre-defining hit (Vampire Survivors, 2021–22) → second-wave variants (Brotato and others pushed VS below 25% share) → consolidation into an oligopoly. Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor was "the last" breakthrough before Megabonk. By May 2025, three games held over 75% of the VS-like market until Megabonk took over. His advice: enter in phase 3, invent a mechanic later games copy (Rogue Genesia's meta-map, 20 Minutes Till Dawn's click-to-fire), or curate the genre's feature tree as DRG: Survivor did ([How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/11/12/the-cycle-of-a-hit-genre/)).
- **Genre institutionalized:** in 2025, more than 450 developers coordinated to get a Steam tag created, and a poll drew over 8,000 responses. In May 2026 Valve added a "Bullet Heaven" tag (PC Gamer, May 18, 2026). Steam has run Bullet Heaven fests in Oct 2023, Dec 2024 and Dec 2025 ([Wikipedia: Vampire Survivors–like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like)).
- **Critical view of the clone wave:** RPS's Ed Thorn said most clones fail to separate the player from visual clutter, while the A.V. Club defended iteration ([Wikipedia: VS-like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors%E2%80%93like); [PC Gamer, "So, so many Vampire Survivors clones…"](https://www.pcgamer.com/so-so-many-vampire-survivors-clones-came-out-this-year/)). Kotaku said the world doesn't need many more auto-shooters but welcomed Megabonk ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/the-best-steam-deck-games-ever-megabonk-balatro-vampire-survivors-2000667367), via search summary).
- **Bullet heaven is still drawing interest in 2026:** Hellforged, a bullet heaven, ranked #7 and Guildrun, a roguelike autobattler, ranked #1 on GDCo's chart of top Steam demos for unreleased games ([GDCo, Aug 21, 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).

### Inferences
- The space is crowded but still growing. Revenue has grown faster (Alinea's +80%) than release counts (+28%), which suggests demand is not saturated. It is concentrated, though: each year about 5–10 roguelites take most of the money. The base rate for an average entry is poor.
- Recent winners share a clear pattern: a **one-sentence twist on a familiar frame**, legible in a GIF, plus a low price and a "one more run" loop. Megabonk = Vampire Survivors in 3D. Ball x Pit = Arkanoid as a survivors-like with a city. Balatro = poker as a roguelike. CloverPit = slots. A "Death Must Die + Dead Cells" pitch alone is a blend of two references, not a twist. It needs a sharper hook (see Q6).
- Tiny teams still break out: Megabonk (solo, about 13 months), Backpack Battles (2 people), Vampire Survivors (solo), Balatro (solo), Ball x Pit (about 7 people). AI-assisted solo development is plausible on the engineering side. The bottleneck is likely art, animation and game-feel quality, plus marketing reach.
- Raijin's estimates appear to undercount relative to official milestones (Halls of Torment, Brotato). Treat its Death Must Die figure of about 563K as a floor-ish ballpark.
- Death Must Die is a commercially solid but mid-tier comp: about $3M estimated gross, a single-player loot ARPG still in Early Access after nearly 3 years. That leaves a gap for a co-op or more polished take on its formula (see Q5 and Q6).

## 2. Go-to-market: pricing, Early Access vs 1.0, demos and Next Fest, wishlists, publishers, creators

### Takeaway
Plan around wishlist and conversion benchmarks, but know that breakouts are driven by virality and creators, not by wishlists alone. GDCo's median first-week conversion is about 0.15× (0.10× for games above $10), and two-thirds of Steam games end with 10k wishlists or fewer. Next Fest amplifies existing demand rather than creating it, and the effect is modest outside the top 25. Early Access is a valid way to fund and develop, but 1.0 rarely beats the Early Access launch month, so the first Early Access launch must be the "real" launch. Publisher deals average about 58% developer share with an advance (median 50%) and 68–70% without one.

#### Early Access vs direct 1.0
- Among games that left Early Access in 2025 (excluding those with fewer than 5,000 Early Access copies), only **20%** earned more in their first 30 days after 1.0 than in their first Early Access month. Median 1.0-month revenue was **40%** of the first Early Access month.
  - Examples: Supermarket Simulator −95%, Backpack Battles −87%, Slime Rancher 2 −85%. News Tower and Mars First Logistics more than doubled.
  - Carless's view: treat 1.0 as one more marketing beat. It matters more if the game has rarely been discounted and the update is major.
  - Source: [Game World Observer on GDCo, Dec 10, 2025](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/12/10/according-to-gamediscoverco-most-games-on-steam-earn-less-after-leaving-early-access-than-they-do-at-launch).
- Successful Early Access roguelites in 2023–2026 include Hades II (about 16 months, over 2M copies estimated during Early Access), Slay the Spire 2, Backpack Battles, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor, Brotato, Halls of Torment, Ravenswatch and Death Must Die. Direct 1.0 hits include Megabonk, Ball x Pit, Absolum and Mewgenics (sources as in Q1).

#### Wishlists: benchmarks and conversion
- **Distribution** of Steam releases over a 12-month window ([VG Insights via wnhub, Jul 23, 2025](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-48332)):
  - 29% had fewer than 1k wishlists.
  - 37% had 1k–10k, so about two-thirds stayed at 10k or below.
  - 6% (301 games) passed 100k.
  - 22 games reached at least 1M.
- **Conversion:**
  - The median is 0.15× in the first week for games with more than 25k wishlists, and 0.10× above $10. It varies "by an order of magnitude".
  - Underperformers had a median 67% user score in their first 7 days versus 91% for strong performers.
  - Underperformers had been on Steam pre-release longer: 411 days on average versus 214.
  - Top converters were friendslop games: Peak 29.29×, Mage Arena 8.67×, R.E.P.O. 7.51×.
  - Source: [GDCo via GameDev Reports, Nov 3, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam).
  - An earlier GDCo figure was about 0.17× for games with more than 10k wishlists (search summary). Smaller games convert at a higher ratio: Bramble Royale's 17% week-1 conversion beat GDCo's 10.5% median estimate but fell short of the 29% GDCo found for games under 10k wishlists ([GDCo, Apr 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/why-did-this-excellent-roguelike)).
- **Longer-window conversions (Alinea):**
  - Ball x Pit: about 26% at 90 days.
  - Dispatch: about 25% at 3 months.
  - No, I'm Not a Human: about 15% of 189k.
  - Jump Space: about 10%.
  - Dead as Disco: about 5% at 30 days.
  - Pioner: about 1%, hurt by technical problems.
  - Source: [Alinea, Jun 23, 2026](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/steam-next-fests-winners-and-why).

#### Demos and Steam Next Fest
- **Feb 2025 Next Fest:** 2,244 participants. Zukowski surveyed 208 of them. Tiers are by wishlists earned during the fest (Diamond 10k+, Bronze under 1k).
  - Diamond: median about 15k demo players and 15,037 wishlists, 68% of them from people who never played the demo.
  - Bronze: 455 wishlists, 88% from non-players.
  - Pre-fest wishlists predict the fest outcome. Median wishlists earned during the fest were 462 for games starting with 0–999, 1,513 for 1k–9.9k, 6,360 for 10k–99.9k, and 23,731 for 100k+.
  - No game starting below 1k wishlists reached Diamond.
  - Source: [Zukowski data as reproduced by presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/analyzing-next-fest-results).
- Games whose demo launched well before Next Fest reportedly earned about 2.5× more fest wishlists. Zukowski's Feb 2026 survey median was 806 wishlists gained, while GDCo estimates the median across all demos is nearer 200, because survey respondents skew toward heavy marketers ([Steam Page Analyzer summary](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-next-fest-results); search summary, not verified against the primary).
- **June 2026 Next Fest:** over 4,300 demos. Alinea's view: the impact is "modest even for the biggest games, and minimal for anything outside the top 25". The fest amplifies rather than creates demand. Track demo playtime and the demo-to-wishlist attachment rate ([Alinea, Jun 23, 2026](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/steam-next-fests-winners-and-why)).
- Long-lived, frequently updated demos work for this genre. Backpack Battles kept its demo live for about 9 months with weekly patches and peaked at 18,960 demo CCU ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)). Ball x Pit's June 2025 demo was the press's Next Fest standout ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/ball-x-pit-is-steam-next-fest-demo/)).

#### Publisher vs self-publishing
- **Voyer Law dataset** (100+ developer–publisher agreements, 2017–2025; the author warns it "may not fully reflect current realities") ([GameDev Reports, May 12, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/voyer-law-publishing-agreements-in)):
  - Developer revenue share: with an advance, average 58.2% and median 50% (range 2.5–90%). Without an advance, average 67.9% and median 70%. Console-porting deals, average 63.1% and median 60%.
  - Advances: average $674,861, median $300k, range $20k to over $6M. Paid against milestones in 79.7% of deals.
  - Recoupment comes from the revenue share in 93.9% of deals, and happens before the developer's first payout in 48.4%.
  - The developer keeps the IP in 96.4% of advance deals.
  - Sequel rights appear in 63.5% of advance deals, mostly exclusive.
  - Average term: 6.84 years for advance deals, 3.7 years for console deals.
  - A different secondary summary described the sample as 30 agreements. The fetched source says over 100.
- Recoupment structure (before or after the split, from whose share) matters more than the headline split ([Rami Ismail, "Upfronts, Guarantees, Recoups"](https://ltpf.ramiismail.com/upfronts-guarantees-recoups/); [PC Gamer on good and bad deals](https://www.pcgamer.com/what-a-good-and-bad-indie-game-publishing-deal-looks-like/); both via search summary).
- **Recent outcomes:**
  - Self-published breakouts: Megabonk, Mewgenics (PC), Slay the Spire 2, Death Must Die.
  - Publisher-backed breakouts: Ball x Pit (Devolver, which put on a single-game showcase), Balatro (Playstack), Absolum (Dotemu), Ravenswatch (Nacon), and Backpack Battles (publisher-run Asia localization and influencer push) (sources in Q1).

#### Creator and streamer appeal, viral mechanics
- YouTubers prefer games they can "main" and go deep on ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/why-did-this-excellent-roguelike)).
- Co-op games are "super streamable" because of voice interplay, and "the next game to play with your friends" is an active buyer market ([GDCo, Aug 21, 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).
- Megabonk was driven by devlog videos and Shorts, then by top streamers ([Esports Insider NL](https://esportsinsider.com/nl/2025/10/03/megabonk-ontploft-in-populariteit-1-miljoen-verkopen-in-2-weken-tijd); [YouTube fandom wiki](https://youtube.fandom.com/wiki/Vedinad)). Halls of Torment was lifted by Asmongold ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/asmongold-twitch-steam-hit-aus-stuttgart-halls-of-torment/)). Backpack Battles grew through VTubers and Bilibili, sending keys 2 months early with an embargo to release day ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)).
- A shareable concept beats a long Steam presence. Bombanana went from a page live only 25 days before Next Fest to a top result on the strength of its hook and organic marketing ([Alinea](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/steam-next-fests-winners-and-why)).
- Bain & Co found two-thirds of players prefer familiar games or sequels, and only one in five seeks new titles ([cited in GDCo, Aug 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).

### Inferences
- **Price:** a pixel-art action roguelite in the Death Must Die / Dead Cells mold can credibly charge $14.99–19.99 at 1.0 if the content depth is real. A lower Early Access price ($9.99–14.99) that ramps up later is an accepted norm. Pricing below $10 boosts conversion and virality, as with Megabonk and Ball x Pit, but caps revenue per unit.
- **Early Access:** the GDCo data argues for a single big launch: either a polished Early Access with a complete core loop, or 1.0 directly. Do not expect 1.0 to rescue a weak Early Access. A playable demo should go up months before Next Fest, and the demo-to-wishlist rate should be treated as the key metric.
- **Wishlist targets:** using the 0.10–0.15× first-week medians, about 50k wishlists suggests roughly 5–7.5k first-week units. A break-even plan for a solo developer should not depend on a Megabonk-style viral multiplier.
- **Creators:** systems that create emergent, clip-able moments (screen-filling synergies, absurd builds, run-ending failures), plus depth a creator can "main", are the creator hooks. A devlog channel is a proven channel for a solo developer (Megabonk).
- **Publisher:** for a Russia-based solo developer, a publisher can solve payment and entity, localization, console and PR problems, at the cost of about 30–50% of revenue (Voyer medians) and recoupment. Self-publishing keeps 70% of net after Steam's cut but requires the developer to do everything.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025–26 data on how Early Access vs direct 1.0 affects lifetime revenue specifically for roguelites.
- The Feb 2026 Zukowski survey numbers and the 2.5× demo-timing figure come only from a secondary summary.
- No sourced data on Megabonk's or Ball x Pit's pre-launch wishlist totals beyond the developer's "80,000" remark.
- No quantified data on how much short-form video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) contributes to wishlists.

## 3. Platform strategy: Steam Deck, consoles, mobile, subscriptions

### Takeaway
Steam on PC is the primary market. **Steam Deck matters disproportionately for roguelites.** Over the last 12 months the most-played Deck games included Slay the Spire 2 (#1), Hades II (#2), Megabonk (#4), Ball x Pit (#9) and Balatro (top 10). For 2D or pixel action roguelites, majority-Deck review shares run at 10–13%, against a 0.55% median across all tags. Consoles are typically a second wave, 6–18 months after a PC hit, through porters or publishers. Quotes vary widely, and costs rise for custom engines. Premium mobile works for prestige ports (Dead Cells mobile sold 5M), but survivors-likes on mobile are dominated by free-to-play. Subscription deals (Game Pass, PS Plus, Netflix) are common add-ons for indie roguelites; the money involved is private.

### Cited Findings
- **Steam Deck** ([GDCo, Sep 25, 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-are-steam-decks-biggest-2026)):
  - Installed base estimated at 6–8M, versus 23M Switch 2 units shipped.
  - Deck playtime is about 2–4% of many publishers' catalogs, but 10–20%+ for some titles.
  - Valve's top-played Deck chart for the last 12 months: Slay the Spire 2 #1, Hades II #2, Megabonk #4, Ball x Pit #9, with Balatro also in the top 10.
  - Share of reviews written by majority-Deck players: UFO 50 19.2%, Mina the Hollower 13.7%, The Rogue Prince of Persia 13.5%.
  - Genre medians: 0.55% overall; 2D platformer 2.3%; roguelike deckbuilder and metroidvania 2%; FPS 0.22%; MMO 0.1%.
  - Corroborated by [Game World Observer, Sep 28, 2026](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/09/28/games-with-which-tags-are-popular-on-the-steam-deck-statistics-from-gamediscoverco).
- An earlier GDCo-relayed estimate: the Deck is about 0.7% of Steam users but about 2.75% of Steam playtime ([GameDev Reports summarizing GDCo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-which-games-are-successful), via search summary).
- Megabonk shipped native on Linux and was Deck Verified at launch ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/10/megabonk-is-a-mega-success-selling-1-million-in-2-weeks-after-being-repeatedly-told-to-delay-it/)).
- **Console release volume in 2025:** Switch 1/2 over 3,100 releases (+8%), PS4/PS5 over 1,300 (+4%), Xbox Series about 900 (−20%). Sports and AAA dominate the PlayStation charts, and Nintendo's audience differs noticeably from Steam's ([GDCo via GameDev Reports, Dec 29, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-most-successful-new)).
- **Port timing examples:**
  - Dead Cells: consoles at 1.0, after 15 months of PC Early Access; iOS one year later, Android two years later ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cells)).
  - Vampire Survivors: Xbox 3 weeks after 1.0, Switch 10 months after, PS about 2 years after ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)).
  - Risk of Rain 2: console Early Access 5 months after PC Early Access ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_of_Rain_2)).
  - Halls of Torment: consoles announced about 2 years after PC Early Access, once it passed 1M ([iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2025/09/03/indi-xit-v-stile-vampire-survivors-skoro-doberetsia-do-playstation-i-xbox-prodan-million-kopii-halls.html)).
  - Mewgenics: consoles 7 months after the PC launch, through publisher Nicalis ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewgenics)).
  - The Rogue Prince of Persia: consoles with 1.0, day one on Game Pass and PS Plus, and Switch 4 months later ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/08/the-rogue-prince-of-persia-now-available-for-ps5-xbox-series-and-pc-coming-to-switch-2-and-switch-in-2025)).
  - Absolum launched simultaneously on PC, Switch and PS, without Xbox ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/10/the-awesome-absolum-smashes-through-200000-sales-in-its-first-week)).
- **Porting costs** (vendor quotes, so treat them as sales pitches) ([PolyCrunch](https://polycrunch.games/how-much-does-console-porting-cost-for-indie-game-developers/); [Klabater](https://klabater.com/en/video-game-porting/); [Noble Robot](https://noblerobot.com/porting-publishing); [gamedo.live 2026 guide](https://gamedo.live/news/how-to-port-indie-game-consoles-2026/); [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/7120686); all via search summary):
  - About $4–9k per platform for simple Unity or GameMaker games, higher with leaderboards or online play.
  - About $20k per title in one developer's quote.
  - $10–50k+ per platform for Godot or custom engines.
  - Porter-publisher models: 10–30% revenue share, a 50/50 split until costs are recouped, or porting at the porter's cost in exchange for royalties.
  - Certification takes 2–6 weeks, plus devkit costs.
- In the Voyer data, console-porting deals left the developer an average 63.1% and median 60% share ([GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/voyer-law-publishing-agreements-in)).
- **Mobile, premium model:** Dead Cells mobile (Playdigious) launched on iOS in Aug 2019 and Android in Jun 2020 at $9.99 with no ads or IAP. It passed 5M sales in about 3 years, over 2M of them in China within about 5 months of the local launch. It joined Netflix Games on Oct 31, 2023 with all DLC ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/dead-cells/mobile-sales); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/dead-cells-mobile-has-surpassed-2-million-units-in-china); [TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/2019/05/07/dead-cells-mobile-price-controls-motion-twin-port/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cells)).
- **Mobile, free-to-play model:**
  - Vampire Survivors mobile (Dec 2022) is free with optional rewarded ads and no IAP. Poncle rushed it out to counter clones after failing to find a publisher willing to use non-predatory monetization ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/vampire-survivors-developer-takes-new-approach-to-monetisation)).
  - It reached 1M downloads in week 1 and 5.1M in about 3 months with no paid user acquisition; no revenue data is public ([MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/vampire-survivors-mobile-hits-5m-downloads/)).
  - Habby's IAP-driven Survivor.io had made over $170M by early 2023, per AppMagic (same source).
- **Subscriptions:**
  - Ball x Pit was on Game Pass from day one, and its 2M sales exclude Game Pass ([wnhub](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-51689)).
  - Brotato logged over 1M Game Pass downloads ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/noticias/776431/Este-juego-la-rompio-en-Xbox-Game-Pass-devs-celebran-mas-de-1-millon-de-descargas)).
  - Big Walk (co-op) had about 3.5M players, about 2M of them from a PS Plus Monthly inclusion at launch ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).
  - Day-one Game Pass is "a gamble" for indies: deal amounts are private, and developer experiences vary ([XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/day-one-game-pass-great-consumers-gamble-indie-developers/)).
  - In 2025, every top-20 Game Pass title had over 1M downloads, and 9 of the 20 came from Microsoft's own studios ([GDCo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-most-successful-new)).
- **Engine risk:** Slay the Spire 2 moved from Unity to Godot mid-development after the 2023 runtime-fee controversy, reworking about 2 years of work ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2)).

### Inferences
- For this team, a "maximally native" stack is a **Deck and Linux advantage**: low latency, small footprint, native Linux builds, and performance on handhelds. It is also a **console-porting liability**: no off-the-shelf console export, higher porter quotes, and the work of certification and platform SDKs. If consoles matter, budget for a porter-publisher deal, or keep the platform layer thin and portable from day one.
- Design for handhelds from the start: readable pixel art at 800p, 15–25 minute runs, instant suspend and resume (the deterministic simulation makes snapshots cheap), and Steam Input. This matches where the genre's playtime actually goes.
- Mobile should not be a launch target. If the PC game hits, a premium port through a specialist (the Playdigious model) or a subscription deal (Netflix, Apple Arcade) are proven paths. Free-to-play survivors-likes on mobile are a different, user-acquisition-driven business.
- A Game Pass or PS Plus deal is opportunistic. Ball x Pit shows it can coexist with strong sales, but the terms are unknown and hard for a small Russia-based developer to access directly.

- No sourced roguelite-specific share of Steam sales (as opposed to playtime or reviews) coming from Deck owners.
- No public Game Pass or PS Plus payment amounts for indie roguelites.
- No current (2025–26) download or revenue data for Vampire Survivors, Brotato or Balatro mobile.
- No sourced porting costs specific to custom or native C++/Rust engines, beyond the general $10–50k+ range.

## 4. Monetization norms for premium roguelites

### Takeaway
The norm is premium-only, with no microtransactions. Revenue comes from a low-to-mid base price, then 1–4 paid content expansions over years mixed with free updates, plus an inexpensive soundtrack and cross-promotional bundles. DLC attach rates are typically low single digits, with strong cases at 8–21%. Communities accept paid DLC that adds substantial content and pre-announced Early Access price increases. They punish thin or buggy launches and disruptive balance changes with mixed reviews and review bombs.

### Cited Findings
- **Dead Cells model:** over about 5 years after launch it alternated four paid DLCs (The Bad Seed in Feb 2020, Fatal Falls Jan 2021, The Queen and the Sea Jan 2022, Return to Castlevania Mar 2023) with many free updates, including a free boss-rush mode in Oct 2022. Sales grew from 2M (2019) to 5M (2021) to over 10M (2023) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cells)).
- **Vampire Survivors model:**
  - Paid expansions: Legacy of the Moonspell (Dec 2022), Tides of the Foscari (Apr 2023), and Legacy of the Bloodmoon (listed for Aug 2026).
  - Licensed crossover DLC: Among Us (Dec 2023), Contra (May 2024), Castlevania (Oct 2024).
  - Free crossovers: SaGa (Apr 2025), Balatro (Oct 2025).
  - Local co-op added Aug 2023 and online co-op Oct 2025; plus a VR version and an animated or film adaptation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)).
- **Risk of Rain 2:** four paid DLCs through Oct 2026 (Mar 2022, Aug 2024, Nov 2025, Oct 2026) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_of_Rain_2)).
- **DLC attach-rate estimates** (Alinea, Steam, May 14, 2026, via aggregator [rec0ded88](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/roguelike-genre/)):

  | DLC | Price | Copies | Base game | Peak attach |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Monster Train 2 DLC | $9.99 | 112k | 525k | over 21% |
  | Barony DLC | $7.99 | about 200k | about 1M | over 19% |
  | Ravenswatch: Merlin | $7.99 | 106k (about $662k) | 1.2M | 9% |
  | CloverPit: Unholy Fusion | $2.99 | 129k | 1.7M | about 8% |

  Alinea puts the general baseline "in the low single digits".
- **Soundtrack and supporter packs:**
  - Ball x Pit sells its soundtrack for €7.99 ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)).
  - In one case, a supporter pack with a 2.59% purchase rate earned $11,531, while a soundtrack with a 2.8% rate earned $60,980 ([Mein-MMO EN](https://mein-mmo.de/en/steam-money-cheat-macht-fans-gluecklich,1559825)).
  - Soundtrack attach is low single digits, and the revenue split should be set in the composer contract ([bugnet.io](https://bugnet.io/blog/is-a-soundtrack-dlc-worth-it-for-indie-devs)).
  - An example supporter pack priced at $10 ([wnhub on Into the Radius](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-43549)).
  - All three sources are weaker; treat as indicative.
- **Bundles:** Backpack Battles' "complete the set" bundles with Slay the Spire and Brotato made up about 35% of its sales ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)). Death Must Die runs bundles with 20 Minutes Till Dawn, Brotato and SWORN ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2334730/Death_Must_Die/)). Ball x Pit runs bundles with Vampire Survivors, CloverPit and Monsters are Coming! ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)).
- **What gets punished:**
  - Slay the Spire 2 was review-bombed twice over balance patches, mostly by Chinese players, and once over a credits controversy ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2)).
  - Wizard of Legend 2's Early Access drew mixed reviews over difficulty, thin content and bugs ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2193540/wizard_of_legend_2)).
  - GDCo found underperformers' first-week user score median was 67% versus 91% for strong performers ([GDCo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam)).
- **Mobile ethics as positioning:** Poncle's "free-for-real" mobile model (no IAP) was framed against predatory monetization ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/vampire-survivors-developer-takes-new-approach-to-monetisation)).

### Inferences
- A sensible plan:
  - $14.99–19.99 base price, with a lower announced Early Access price if going through Early Access.
  - Soundtrack DLC at about $5–8 at launch.
  - Optionally a cosmetic supporter pack, but no gameplay-affecting purchases.
  - 1–3 substantial paid expansions (new biome, heroes, mode) at $5–10, interleaved with free updates.
  - Bundles with adjacent indie games from day one (Death Must Die, Brotato and Halls of Torment are natural partners).
- Deterministic simulation plus bot balancing is directly relevant to avoiding balance-patch backlash. Patches can be simulated against a large corpus of bot runs before shipping, and seeded challenge modes can stay stable across versions by versioning the ruleset (inference from the review-bomb examples).

### Gaps
- No primary (non-aggregator) DLC attach data for action roguelites specifically (Dead Cells, Vampire Survivors and Risk of Rain 2 DLC sales were not found).
- No sourced data on cosmetic DLC performance in premium roguelites.
- No sourced cases of community backlash against paid DLC in this genre. I recall reports about Risk of Rain 2's Seekers of the Storm patch problems in 2024, but the fetched source did not cover them, so they are excluded.

## 5. Co-op: effect on sales and retention, and its cost

### Takeaway
Co-op is the strongest commercial tailwind on Steam right now. Multiplayer games took the top 6 sales slots among 2025's new releases, the top wishlist converters were co-op "friendslop", and audiences overlap heavily (53% of Big Walk players also played Risk of Rain 2). Co-op action roguelites sell durably: Risk of Rain 2 sold over 4M on PC by 2021, Ravenswatch about 1.2M is estimated, and Slay the Spire 2 and Vampire Survivors both added co-op. However, 2025's three biggest action-roguelite breakouts (Megabonk, Ball x Pit, Hades II) were **single-player**. Co-op therefore multiplies reach but is not required to break out. It is expensive: netcode, sync, testing, and higher porting and online-service costs.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 Steam top new releases:** multiplayer games took the top 6 slots of the top 20 by units. Friendslop titles (R.E.P.O., PEAK, Schedule I, RV There Yet?, Mage Arena) were a major theme ([GDCo via GameDev Reports, Dec 29, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-most-successful-new); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steam-top-selling-2025-friendslop-rpgs-sales-2000654157)).
- **Highest wishlist-to-sale conversions** were all co-op: Peak 29.29×, Mage Arena 8.67×, R.E.P.O. 7.51× ([GDCo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam)).
- **Big Walk** (Aug 2026) sold 1M in about 6 days.
  - Its players overlapped with PEAK 75%, Lethal Company 67%, **Risk of Rain 2 53%** and Helldivers 2 51%.
  - Positive reviews often mention "friends".
  - Co-op games are "super streamable" because of voice interplay.
  - Source: [GDCo, Aug 21, 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in).
- **Risk of Rain 2** (4-player online co-op): 650k players in its first Early Access week, about 150k of them through a buy-one-get-one promotion. It sold over 1M in its first month and over 4M on PC by Mar 2021. Gearbox bought the IP in Nov 2022, and paid DLCs continue into 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_of_Rain_2)).
- **Ravenswatch** (up to 4-player co-op): 500k players by Sep 2024 and about 1.2M Steam base copies by May 2026 (Alinea estimate). Online play on console requires a paid Game Pass Core or Ultimate subscription ([Game Developer PR](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/ravenswatch-now-available-on-pc-); [rec0ded88/Alinea](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/roguelike-genre/); [Xbox store page](https://xbox.com/en-US/games/store/1/9P4NX9RD7FRG), via search summary).
- **Slay the Spire 2** shipped 4-player co-op in Early Access, a first for the series. Enemies scale and the run ends only if all players fall ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2)).
- **Vampire Survivors** added local co-op for up to 4 players in Aug 2023, then online co-op in beta in Aug 2025, released fully in Oct 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)).
- **Absolum** offers 2-player local and online co-op, and **Windblown** made co-op a design goal, unlike Dead Cells ([Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/article/17800/dotemu-s-first-original-ip-is-absolum-and-it-s-heating-up-the-sales-charts.html); [Wikipedia: Windblown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windblown)).
- **Single-player counter-evidence:** Megabonk (single-player only, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk)), Ball x Pit (single-player tag, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)), Death Must Die (single-player, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2334730/Death_Must_Die/)) and Mewgenics.
- **Cost signals:**
  - Porters price online features (leaderboards, online play) above base porting quotes ([PolyCrunch](https://polycrunch.games/how-much-does-console-porting-cost-for-indie-game-developers/), via search summary).
  - Wizard of Legend 2's co-op Early Access drew bug and performance complaints ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2193540/wizard_of_legend_2)).
  - Vampire Survivors took about 2 years to go from local to online co-op ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)).

### Inferences
- For this team, co-op is the hook whose cost is unusually low. Deterministic simulation with bit-exact replays and a relay server is the classic basis for lockstep or rollback co-op. Desync detection comes almost free through state hashes, and replays double as bug reports. The main costs that remain are real-time latency handling for action combat (rollback or prediction is needed for melee and dash) and NAT and relay hosting.
- Recommended scope: 2–3 player online co-op (as Windblown planned), designed in from day one rather than retrofitted (as Vampire Survivors did over 2 years), plus a strong single-player mode. Steam Remote Play Together can serve as a cheap local-co-op stopgap. A buy-one-get-one or "friend pass" launch promotion has precedent (Risk of Rain 2 sold about 150k copies through one).
- Co-op broadens the creator angle: friends' streams and voice comedy fit the friendslop wave, while solo builds remain clip-able.

### Gaps
- No public A/B-style data isolating co-op's effect on sales or retention in action roguelites. The evidence is correlational: top sellers, overlap, conversion outliers.
- No sourced engineering-cost estimates for adding online co-op to an action roguelite.
- I could not verify whether Megabonk, Ball x Pit or Death Must Die added co-op after launch.

## 6. Differentiation: 8–12 hook ideas with evidence of demand or gaps, evaluated for this team

### Takeaway
The strongest positioning for this team combines a legible core (a pixel-art action roguelite with Diablo-style loot, the Death Must Die frame) with one or two signature systems that only this team can build cheaply. Those are **draft-your-party with hero synergy and chemistry** and **deterministic social competition** (seeded dailies, ghost races, async PvP against other players' builds), optionally with small-party co-op. Avoid a vanilla survivors-like (an oligopoly) and a vanilla deckbuilder ("fatigue"). Pick one hook that reads in a 5-second clip and one that sustains depth for creators who "main" the game.

### Cited Findings
Each hook below lists the evidence of demand or of a gap, with sources.

1. **Co-op (2–4 player) pixel ARPG roguelite with Diablo-style loot**, i.e. "Death Must Die with friends".
   - Death Must Die is single-player only, English only, still in Early Access, and an estimated about 563K-copy success ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2334730/Death_Must_Die/); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/2334730/Death_Must_Die)).
   - Co-op action-roguelite comps sell durably: Risk of Rain 2 over 4M on PC ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_of_Rain_2)), Ravenswatch about 1.2M estimated ([rec0ded88/Alinea](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/roguelike-genre/)).
   - Co-op converts and streams best ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in); [GDCo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam)).
2. **Draft-your-squad / party with hero synergy and chemistry**: a pre-run or between-floor hero draft, tag-team hero swapping, and party-wide synergies (the team's aegis-draft heritage).
   - Mewgenics, built on squad breeding and composition, sold over 1M in its first week ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewgenics)).
   - Ball x Pit includes recruitable heroes with upgradable mechanics ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)).
   - Guildrun, a roguelike autobattler, was GDCo's #1 unreleased demo in Aug 2026 ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).
   - Familiar frame plus twist is what players prefer: two-thirds favor familiar games (Bain, [via GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)).
3. **Async PvP against other players' recorded builds or runs ("ghost builds")**.
   - Backpack Battles sold 640k in its first month with async PvP and a ranked mode ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)).
   - GDCo says mashups like The Bazaar and Backpack Battles outperform vanilla deckbuilders ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/why-did-this-excellent-roguelike)).
4. **Deterministic daily or weekly seeded runs with verified leaderboards and downloadable replays**, and ghost races on the same seed.
   - Demand evidence is indirect. The genre's top titles are heavily replayed: a GamesRadar headline cited on Wikipedia reported Slay the Spire 2 at 3M units and over 25M runs shortly after launch ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2)). No sourced retention data for daily modes was found (see Gaps).
5. **Live versus or "battle royale of runs"**: N players on the same seed at the same time, where kills send curses or elites to rivals, as 1v1 duels or the 18-player arena already prototyped.
   - Indirect support: the co-op and friend-group market and streamability ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-big-walks-1m-sales-usher-in)). No sourced comp in action roguelites, so this is an untested gap with high risk.
6. **Hub or base-building meta layer**.
   - Ball x Pit's city of New Ballbylon has 70+ buildings, and the game reached 2M+ sales against an internal 300k first-year expectation ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/); [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/56013-ball-x-pit-more-than-tripled-devolver-digital-s-full-year-sales-expectations-in-just)).
7. **Genre mashup with a universally legible non-roguelite mechanic** (an inventory or backpack grid in action combat, cards driving real-time attacks, pinball or slots as a loot layer).
   - Balatro sold over 5M ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro)), CloverPit over 1.7M estimated ([rec0ded88/Alinea](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/roguelike-genre/)), Ball x Pit 2M ([wnhub](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-51689)) and Backpack Battles 640k in its first month ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)).
   - Zukowski's advice: invent the mechanic others copy ([HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/11/12/the-cycle-of-a-hit-genre/)).
8. **Survivors-like with real action skill** (manual aim, dodge or parry, Dead Cells-style melee) to escape the auto-shooter oligopoly.
   - Market concentration (three games held over 75% until Megabonk) ([HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/11/12/the-cycle-of-a-hit-genre/)).
   - Kotaku: "the world doesn't need many more auto-shooters" ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/the-best-steam-deck-games-ever-megabonk-balatro-vampire-survivors-2000667367)).
   - Action roguelites over $1M average $8.4M ([games.gg/GDCo](https://games.gg/news/steam-revenue-distribution-by-genre/)).
9. **Boss-rush or arena-focused mode** (short, skill-ranked sessions).
   - Weak evidence: Dead Cells added a free boss-rush mode late in its life ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cells)). No sales data.
10. **Handheld-first design** (native performance, 15–25 minute runs, suspend anywhere, Linux-native, Deck Verified).
    - Roguelites dominate Valve's Deck top-played list. The Rogue Prince of Persia's majority-Deck review share is 13.5% ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-are-steam-decks-biggest-2026)), and Megabonk was native Linux and Deck Verified ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/10/megabonk-is-a-mega-success-selling-1-million-in-2-weeks-after-being-repeatedly-told-to-delay-it/)).
11. **Asia-first localization and community** (Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean at launch; Bilibili and VTuber outreach).
    - Simplified Chinese is 31.39% of Steam survey users versus 34.20% English (Sep 2026) ([Steam HW survey](https://store.steampowered.com/hwsurvey/Steam-Hardware-Software-Survey-Welcome-to-Steam)).
    - Backpack Battles: 48% of sales from China ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)). Bramble Royale: 18% China, 9% Japan ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/why-did-this-excellent-roguelike)). Slay the Spire 2: 87% positive in Simplified Chinese reviews and China-driven review bombs ([rec0ded88/Alinea](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/roguelike-genre/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2)).

    - Vampire Survivors' licensed and free crossovers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)). Backpack Battles' bundles made up 35% of sales ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)).

## 7. Practical note for developers based in Russia/CIS (conditional, high-level)

### Takeaway
Steam does not ban Russian developers, but payouts and fee payment run through banking channels that are largely closed to Russian banks. In practice developers use foreign personal cards or accounts (Kazakhstan and Belarus are often mentioned) or a foreign legal entity with a non-sanctioned USD-capable SWIFT account, or work through a publisher. VK Play is a domestic alternative with low commission, but its scale is smaller and its terms may have changed since 2022. Russian-speaking players are still about 9% of Steam's surveyed users.

### Cited Findings
- **Payout channels:** Valve pays only to SWIFT-capable, USD-capable accounts at non-sanctioned banks, which blocks most direct payouts to Russia. A foreign entity with a foreign or neobank account is a common workaround, and banks may hold payments for 1–2 weeks for compliance checks ([Skillfactory](https://blog.skillfactory.ru/viplaty-razrabotchikam-igr-v-rossii/); [Easy Payments, promotional](https://easypayments.online/blog/kak-poluchat-vyplaty-ot-steam); both via search summary). An older 2022 report said payouts resumed except to sanctioned banks ([Skillbox](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/steam-vozobnovil-vyplaty-razrabotchikam-iz-rossii/)); its current validity is unconfirmed.
- **A developer's first-hand account** (Dec 12, 2025):
  - A Russian Steam account can't serve as the developer account (the Steam Direct payment page won't load), and the $100 Steam Direct fee can't be paid with a Russian card.
  - Developers use foreign personal cards, often Kazakh or Belarusian, or a foreign friend.
  - The tax interview runs through Steam's TaxIdentity flow.
  - The author says Valve has imposed no sanctions; the obstacles are banks and some companies.
  - Source: [DTF](https://dtf.ru/id615524/4528029-kak-vypustit-igru-v-steam-iz-rossii-opyt-razrabotchika).
- **VK Play:** commission cut to 5% including payment fees (June 23, 2022), with up to 350k impressions for new games to test metrics. Individuals can publish ([dev.by](https://devby.io/ru/news/vk-play-snizila-komissiu-dlya-razrabotchikov-igr-do-5); [wnhub RU](https://wnhub.io/ru/news/marketing/item-191); [Mobile-Review](https://mobile-review.com/all/news/fizicheskie-licza-smogut-publikovat-igry-na-vk-play/)). These terms date from 2022; current 2025–26 terms were not found.
- **Audience:** Russian is 9.05% of Steam HW survey users in Sep 2026, third after English (34.20%) and Simplified Chinese (31.39%) ([Steam HW survey](https://store.steampowered.com/hwsurvey/Steam-Hardware-Software-Survey-Welcome-to-Steam)). A 2025 indie deckbuilder still drew 4% of its Steam sales from Russia ([GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/why-did-this-excellent-roguelike)).
- An RBC Companies piece on publishing on Steam from Russia in 2025 exists but could not be accessed (HTTP 401) ([RBC Companies](https://companies.rbc.ru/news/B3OM4zV5A0/kak-opublikovat-igru-v-steam-v-2025-godu-razrabotchikam-iz-rossii/)).

### Inferences
- The cleanest structure for a commercial-grade release is probably a foreign entity in a jurisdiction where the owner can legally and practically bank, with a USD SWIFT account. This also simplifies future publisher, porting, console-platform and Game Pass contracts, which typically require a company. Alternatively, sign with a publisher that handles distribution and pays the developer through a compliant channel. These are inferences; they need a lawyer or accountant, and sanctions and tax compliance must be checked for the specific jurisdictions.
- VK Play is worth listing as a secondary storefront for the Russian audience, but the commercial base remains Steam (global) plus Chinese and Japanese localization.
- Western publishers and console platform holders may run sanctions due diligence on Russia-based teams. This is an inference, not a sourced fact. Budget time for it.
